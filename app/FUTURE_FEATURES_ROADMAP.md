# 🚀 ScribeTok Future Features Roadmap

## 📋 Features NOT Building Yet (But Planned)

### **🎮 Advanced Gamification & Social**
- **Leaderboards:** "Top 10 creators this week"
- **Achievements/Badges:** "First 1M views", "5 viral videos", "10 referrals"
- **Social Feed:** See what videos friends are transcribing
- **Creator Competitions:** Weekly challenges with bonus credits
- **Public Profiles:** Shareable creator stats pages

### **📊 Advanced Analytics & Growth Tracking**
- **Weekly/Monthly Growth Reports:** SMS summaries of follower/view growth
- **/growth Command:** Compare this week vs last week stats
- **Trend Analysis:** "Your videos are trending up 📈"
- **Hashtag Performance:** Which hashtags work best for user
- **Optimal Posting Times:** When their audience is most active
- **Cross-Platform Stats:** YouTube, Instagram integration

### **🤖 AI-Powered Features**
- **Content Suggestions:** "Your audience loves cooking videos!"
- **Viral Prediction:** AI score for video viral potential
- **Script Generator:** AI helps write TikTok scripts
- **Thumbnail Analysis:** AI rates thumbnail effectiveness
- **Voice Clone:** AI voice for script reading
- **Auto-Captions:** Generate TikTok captions from transcripts

### **💰 Advanced Monetization**
- **Creator Economy:** Users can sell transcript access to their content
- **Premium Tiers:** Advanced analytics, priority processing
- **Brand Partnerships:** Connect creators with sponsors
- **Affiliate Program:** Users earn from referrals beyond credits
- **White-Label:** License ScribeTok to agencies/brands

### **🔗 Platform Integrations**
- **YouTube Integration:** Full channel analytics
- **Instagram Reels:** Cross-platform content analysis
- **Spotify/Podcasts:** Transcribe audio content
- **LinkedIn Video:** Professional content insights
- **Twitch Clips:** Gaming content transcription

### **📱 Advanced User Experience**
- **Voice Commands:** Text-to-speech for stats delivery
- **Smart Scheduling:** Auto-send weekly reports
- **Custom Alerts:** "Video hit 100K views!"
- **Bulk Processing:** Upload multiple videos at once
- **Team Accounts:** Agencies managing multiple creators
- **API Access:** Third-party integrations

### **🌍 Scale & Infrastructure**
- **Multi-Language:** Spanish, French, etc. transcription
- **Global Analytics:** Region-specific insights
- **Enterprise Dashboard:** Web portal for power users
- **Advanced Search:** Find transcripts by keyword/topic
- **Data Export:** CSV/JSON exports of all user data
- **Backup/Sync:** Cloud storage integration

### **🎯 Viral Growth Features**
- **Viral Challenges:** ScribeTok-sponsored hashtag challenges
- **Creator Spotlights:** Feature top users in marketing
- **Press Kit Generator:** Auto-create media kits for creators
- **Collaboration Tools:** Connect creators for partnerships
- **Event Integration:** Live event transcription
- **News Alerts:** "Your video was featured in [publication]"

## 🎯 Current MVP Focus (Building Now)

### **✅ Core Creator Stats System**
- `/link @handle` - Connect TikTok accounts
- `/stats` - Personal dashboard (credits, usage, top video)
- `/myvideos` - Show user's top performing videos
- Basic profile linking with URL support

### **✅ Established Foundation** 
- SMS credit system with countdown
- Viral referral system with tracking
- Stripe payment integration
- Multi-API TikTok failover system

## 🎮 Why We're Starting Small

**✅ Validated Demand:** Users already love the core transcription  
**✅ Existing Infrastructure:** TikTok APIs and SMS system ready  
**✅ Clear Value:** Make users feel like professional creators  
**✅ Viral Potential:** Stats are naturally shareable  
**✅ Low Complexity:** Leverage existing data flows  

**The MVP creator stats will validate demand before building the advanced features above.**
